# Consent form — video of your face for training Dwarpal's render detector

> **Draft.** To be reviewed by a lawyer before anyone signs it (with the privacy documents, O5). Adults (18 or over) only.

**Who asks:** the Dwarpal project, run by its owner, [name], [contact email].

**What it's for:** Dwarpal checks that a person in front of a camera is real and not a computer-made face. To learn the
difference, a small program needs examples of both. You would be one of the real examples.

**What happens:**

1. You sit in front of a webcam and follow the usual check's instructions: look ahead, turn your head left and right,
   look up. It takes about a minute; we may film a few short clips in different light.
2. The clips are kept on the project owner's computer, on an encrypted disk. They are **not** published, not put on the
   internet, not shared with anyone, and not kept in Dwarpal's service.
3. The clips are used only to train and test Dwarpal's render detector. The trained model doesn't contain your clips,
   but it is shaped by them, and it may be used in Dwarpal's service, including commercially.

**Your choices:**

- Taking part is voluntary. You can say no now, or stop at any moment while filming.
- You can **withdraw** at any time by writing to [contact email]. Your clips are then deleted within 30 days, and left
  out of every later training. A model already trained can't forget them, but it is replaced at its next training.
- The clips are deleted when they are no longer needed, and at the latest on [date, at most 3 years from today].

**What we keep about you:** the clips, the date, and this form. Not your name in the clips' records: there you are
`person-NNN`.

**Your rights** under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 — to see, correct and erase what we hold
about you, and to complain — are described in Dwarpal's [privacy practice statement](../privacy/practice-statement.md).
