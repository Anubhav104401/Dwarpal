`.gitignore` lists paths that **Git must pretend do not exist**. It never offers them for committing, and `git status` never mentions them. Three kinds of things belong here (§4.1): **secrets**, **things rebuilt automatically**, and **large or licence-restricted data**.

Pattern rules:

- `#` starts a comment;
- a trailing `/` means "a folder with this name, anywhere";
- `*` matches any characters;
- `[abc]` matches one of the listed characters;
- a leading `!` makes an exception — "don't ignore this one after all".
